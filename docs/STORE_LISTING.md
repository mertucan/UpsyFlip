## App icon suggestions

Three directions to sketch; all should read at 48 px and avoid text.

1. **Flipping knife:** the knife at a 35° angle, mid-spin, with a short curved motion trail, on a solid teal background. A thin wooden plank edge at the bottom corner hints at the target. Strongest link to gameplay.
2. **Stuck landing:** the knife standing tip-down in a chunky plank with two or three wood chips flying, warm orange background. Shows the satisfying moment.
3. **Upward arrow blade:** the knife pointing straight up with two flip arcs around it forming an "up" shape, dark navy background with a yellow glow. Most abstract, most distinctive on a crowded home screen.

Shared rules: use the game's palette (white blade, red handle, yellow tip), thick outlines, high contrast, no fine detail, and keep the important part inside the central 66% so Android's icon mask never crops it.
