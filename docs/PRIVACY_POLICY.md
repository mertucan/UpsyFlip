- You can play the Game without creating an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or precise location.

## 3. Information stored on your device

Game progress (scores, coins, gems, unlocked skins, missions, settings and language) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes this information.

## 4. Information processed by third-party services

The Game includes the following third-party services. Each of them collects and processes data under its own privacy policy.

### 4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

Used to show rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and process:

- device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
- IP address, from which approximate (not precise) location is derived;
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
- ad interaction data (which ads were shown and tapped) and diagnostic data.

This data is used to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud.
Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### 4.2 Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

Used to ask users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland for consent to personalized advertising, and to store that choice on the device.

### 4.3 Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config (Google)

Used to understand how the Game is played and to adjust game settings without an update. Firebase may collect:

- an app instance identifier and the Android Advertising ID;
- gameplay events (for example run started, run finished, score, selected skin, ad watched, purchase completed);
- device and app information and approximate location derived from the IP address.

Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 4.4 Google Play Billing (purchases)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details. We receive only a confirmation of what was purchased so the Game can deliver it.
Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. How information is used

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Your choices

- **Ad personalization:** You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland):** You are asked for your choice when you first open the Game. You can change it by clearing the Game's data, which shows the consent message again.
- **Removing ads:** An optional one-time purchase removes interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.
- **Deleting local data:** Uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings.
- **Data held by Google:** Requests to access or delete data processed by the services above can be made through Google's tools linked in their policies. You may also contact us and we will help where we can.

## 7. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, data is processed on the basis of your consent (personalized ads and analytics identifiers), our legitimate interests (running, securing and improving the Game, non-personalized ads, fraud prevention) and performance of a contract (delivering purchases).
